# UX Competitor Research (Bear Liu method)

Pasted notes on Bear Liu's AI-assisted UX competitor research workflow.

---

In this video, creator *Bear Liu* demonstrates how to perform **UX competitor research** in just 20 to 30 minutes by combining AI tools with human judgment. His core philosophy is that AI should handle the **execution** (collecting screens, organizing data), while the human remains responsible for **judgment and final decisions** (1:14, 17:47).

**The workflow involves three key steps:**

1.  **AI-Driven Sourcing (3:31):** Using *Claude* with a specific prompt to generate a list of both **direct competitors** (e.g., *Whoop*, *Peloton*, *Strava*) and **comparators** (products in different categories that solve similar problems, like *Duolingo* or *Spotify*).
2.  **Data Collection & Syncing (6:43):** Using *Mobbin* to build a collection of pricing and paywall screens, then utilizing the *Mobbin* plugin to sync these screens directly into *Figma* (9:42).
3.  **Synthesis & Reporting (11:06):** Running an analysis where *Claude* provides patterns while the user adds personal notes. The final output is structured into **Baselines** (proven patterns) and **Bets** (experimental, high-stakes opportunities) (14:40, 16:35).

*Bear* emphasizes that this observational research is a **pattern-and-gap inventory** of shipped products; it does not replace the necessity of talking to real users (17:50).

## My synthesis: applying this to Crosspaths

**Step 1 — first-pass sourcing** (verify/curate before pulling screens):

*Direct competitors* (meet people IRL / real-time / anonymous rendezvous):
- **Happn** — "crossed paths" dating; the closest analog to the Crosspaths premise.
- **Bumble / Hinge** — the match + "you matched" moment (already in Design Inspiration).
- **Luma, Posh, Partiful** — event framing, RSVP, nightlife vibe (Luma/Posh already filed).
- **Timeleft / Somewhere / 222 / The Feels** — curated meet-strangers-IRL (dinners, small groups).
- **Yubo, Meetup** — meet-new-people and event-based meeting.

*Comparators* (different category, same underlying problem):
- **Uber / Lyft** — "matched with X, meet at Y by time Z" dispatch. Directly maps to Crosspaths' rendezvous card ("Meet Neon Otter at the Mango Cart by 9:48").
- **Airbnb** — trust/safety cues for meeting strangers.
- **Spotify (Blend / Jam)** — shared, real-time sessions between people.
- **Duolingo** — onboarding + lightweight engagement/gamification.
- **BeReal / Marco Polo** — presence and ephemerality (be here now).
- **Pokémon GO** — location-based, get-off-the-couch IRL.

**Step 2 (yours):** pull the relevant screens in Mobbin (matching flow, the "you matched" moment, onboarding, safety/trust, real-time dispatch) → sync to Figma.

**Step 3 — Baselines vs Bets for Crosspaths:**
- *Baselines (adopt proven patterns):* the "you matched" moment; event/RSVP framing; the Uber-style "meet at X by time Y" dispatch; safety cues for meeting a stranger; clear alias/identity.
- *Bets (differentiators, higher risk):* anonymous, no-profile/no-photo matching; physical spot+time rendezvous at a festival; in-page-only notifications (no app store); ephemerality (nothing persists after the event).
